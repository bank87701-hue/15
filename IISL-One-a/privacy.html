1Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. The categories of information we collect include:

a) Information You Provide Directly

  • Account registration details (email address, username, password)
  • Profile information (display name, avatar, bio)
  • Communication data (messages, comments, feedback, support requests)

  • Content you upload, create, or share within the Application

b) Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, pages viewed, referral URLs, crash reports)
  • Usage data (features accessed, interaction patterns, session duration, in-app actions)
  • Location data (approximate geographic location derived from IP address; precise location only with your explicit consent where required by law)

c) Information from Third Parties

  • Social media platform data when you choose to link or sign in via third-party services
  • Analytics and advertising partner data (anonymized where possible)
  • Identity verification data from trusted verification services

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Application and our services
  • Process transactions, send related information (confirmations, invoices, technical notices)
  • Send promotional communications, newsletters, and marketing offers (you may opt out at any time)

  • Facilitate social features and connections with other users where applicable

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR): We process your personal data based on legitimate interests, contractual necessity, your consent, and compliance with legal obligations, as applicable to each processing activity described above.

4Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Regular security assessments, vulnerability scanning, and penetration testing
  • Access controls limiting data availability to authorized personnel only
  • Secure software development lifecycle practices
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy best practices

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will make commercially reasonable efforts to safeguard your data.

5Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.
  • Transaction records are retained for the period required by tax, accounting, and financial regulations (typically 5–7 years).
  • Analytics and log data are retained in aggregated or anonymized form for up to 26 months, after which individually identifiable data is deleted or irreversibly anonymized.
  • Support communications are retained for up to 24 months to enable us to assist you effectively.

When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized so that it can no longer be associated with you.
